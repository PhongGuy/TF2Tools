Built-in crosshairs

The crosshairs that come with TF2Tools were made by players over many years. Each one
belongs to its maker.

- crosshair001 to crosshair200: the Crosshair Pack by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), released
  under Creative Commons CC0.
- The cpma, ql, xhairshadow, tob, wings, seeker and sniper sets: the weapon-specific
  crosshair set from joshuawn's guide on teamfortress.tv
  (https://www.teamfortress.tv/30866/guide-weapon-specific-custom-crosshairs), with
  crosshairs by Watson, the CPMA crosshairs by Quik, the Quake Live crosshairs by povohat
  and the wings by RainofLight.
- crosshair1 to crosshair7: Valve's own crosshairs from Team Fortress 2.
- The rest, such as the collaide, yz50, blanc, Brackets and Dot crosshairs: community
  crosshairs whose makers we could not trace. If you made one, open an issue and we will
  credit you, or take it out if you prefer.

TF2Tools is not made, sponsored, endorsed or licensed by, or affiliated with any of these
makers.
